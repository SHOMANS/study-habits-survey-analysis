# Study Habits and Learning Preferences Across Age Groups

Group coursework for a Data Visualisation module. We ran a short online survey (32 responses) about how people study: how much and when they study, what they use study tools for and how they take notes, whether they prefer to study alone or with others, and how often they use video tutorials and written articles. The notebook checks and prepares the responses, sets the answer types, and compares two age groups (18-24 and 25 and over) using tables and charts.

## Files

- `study_habits_analysis.ipynb` - the analysis notebook (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn)
- `Study Habits and Learning Preferences Across Age Groups.csv` - the survey responses
- `figures/` - all charts as PNG files (300 DPI); files 01-07 are the figures used in the report
